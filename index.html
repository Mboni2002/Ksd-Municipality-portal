Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a business license online?
Simply click on "Register" or "Log In" at the top right, complete the online application form, upload the required documents (ID, proof of address, zoning clearance), and submit. Most applications are processed within 7 working days.
What documents are required to apply?
You'll need a certified ID copy, proof of business/residential address (municipal account or lease), zoning clearance certificate, and tax clearance from SARS. For food-related businesses, a health inspection certificate may be required.
How long does the approval process take?
Standard processing takes 5–7 working days for complete applications. Complex cases (e.g., manufacturing or liquor license) may take up to 14 working days. You can check your status online using your reference number.
Can I renew my license online?
Yes! License renewals are fully digital. Log into your account, go to "My Licenses", and click "Renew". You'll receive a new digital certificate with a QR code valid for the next year.
What is the cost of a business license?
Fees vary by category: small retail (R450), hospitality (R750), transport (R600), and manufacturing (R1,200). Renewals receive a 15% discount. All fees are payable via the secure online portal.
Do I need a physical inspection?
For certain categories like restaurants, daycares, and factories, a municipal health and safety inspection is required. We'll contact you within 3 days to schedule an inspection at your business premises.
